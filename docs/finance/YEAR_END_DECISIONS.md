# Year-End Accounting Decisions Required

The following decisions are not approved and must not be implemented by code until Finance/accounting approval is recorded.

1. Retained earnings account
   - account code
   - account name
   - account type (`equity`)
   - normal balance
   - whether it is platform-wide or entity-specific
2. Is 2026 a short initial operating year?
   - closing treatment for November–December 2026
   - whether January–October 2026 periods are required
3. Opening balances
   - which balance-sheet accounts carry forward
   - treatment of outstanding obligations
   - treatment of unpaid referral reward liabilities
   - whether an opening/transition journal is required
4. Year-end closing procedure
   - who prepares
   - who reviews
   - who approves
   - close and reopen/correction controls
5. Opening journal procedure
   - effective date
   - linkage to prior closing record
   - duplicate prevention
6. Authorized approver for year-end closing.

No retained earnings/equity account currently exists in the production chart of accounts.

Vunio: Agriculture, Marketplace and Business Platform

Vunio is an agriculture platform for farmers, agribusinesses, traders and service providers. It brings farm management, marketplace activity and agricultural business tools together in one place.

Agriculture and farming

Vunio supports agricultural work with tools for managing farm activity, production information and the operational details that help farming teams stay organized.

Marketplace and trading

The Vunio marketplace experience brings agricultural products and trading activity together, helping sellers present what they offer and helping buyers discover agricultural supply.

Vunio is built for agricultural businesses and service providers as well, with tools to connect with the wider agricultural ecosystem and manage the business side of their work.
